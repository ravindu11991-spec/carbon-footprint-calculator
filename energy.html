Renewable Energy

Solar Power

Solar panels generate electricity from sunlight. Harnessing the power of the sun, an unlimited source, reduces carbon dioxide emissions and saves money on energy bills. Solar power is functional even during cloudy weather, thus a practical and maintenance-free option for green living.

Wind Energy

Wind power produces clean energy. Through the utilization of the natural kinetic energy of wind flow, large quantities of electricity can be produced without emissions, both in offshore and onshore wind farms. They also provide great land utilization because agriculture can be practiced under them.

Hydroelectric Energy

The flow of water is sustainable energy. The use of the constant flow of river waters and dams in generating energy makes hydropower a very stable and controllable source of energy. This enables the operator of the grid to change output according to demand at any time.
