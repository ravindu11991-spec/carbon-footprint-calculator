Ethical Fashion

Buy Less

The use of consumption strategies such as "30 Wears" allows consumers to consider the overall value of an item before purchasing it. By valuing quality over trendiness in terms of construction and materials used, overproduction is reduced.

Shop Second-Hand

Interaction with secondary markets, vintage clothing retailers, and digital resale platforms helps to create a circular fashion economy. Using existing apparel decreases the need for virgin resource extraction and limits the creation of waste.

Support Ethical Brands

The consumer purchasing power can act as the economic tool that ensures the companies are held accountable. Consumers should choose products from producers that have been certified by third parties like Fair Trade, GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard), and OEKO-TEX.
