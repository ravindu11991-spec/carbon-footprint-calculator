Why Sustainability Matters

Global warming and pollution impact all people. It is our duty to take immediate action to save the planet for future generations.

Waste Reduction

Reduce pollution through recycling and eliminating single use plastics. Pay particular attention to the “5 R’s” to reduce landfill waste.

Ethical Fashion

Buy brands that utilize eco-friendly materials and fair labor practices. Minimizing fast fashion waste will help to clean the industry.

Renewable Energy

Apply the energy generated from the sun and the wind to prevent the emission of greenhouse gases that pollute the atmosphere. Sustainable energy will save our planet.
